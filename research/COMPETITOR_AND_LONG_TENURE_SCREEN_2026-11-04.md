# Competitor and longer-tenure SIP evidence screen — 4 November 2026

## Decision this research serves

**Question:** Before choosing a first-year Groww SIP problem, are the apparent needs already addressed by Groww or comparable products, and do investors with more than 12 months' experience reveal a mechanism we might otherwise miss?

This is a **bounded, purposive screen**, not a systematic corpus or market-share study. It does not establish frequency, effect on continuation, or a winning segment. Public user accounts, company capability pages, editorial explanations, and industry counts are separate lanes. No survey responses were used in this pass.

After the initial comparison, four additional targeted web queries sought a first-year Groww account and longer-tenure accounts. Results were leads, not admitted records: the visible first-year hits were mainly Reddit, generic advice or simulated examples; the inspected non-Reddit account below was older and on Coin. This is **four queries, zero newly admitted direct-target episodes**, not a population finding or a claim that none exist. Reddit hits were not added to the corpus under the [current Responsible Builder Policy](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/42728983564564-Responsible-Builder-Policy) noted in the prior web-first decision.

## Counting and cohort discipline

- [AMFI's September 2026 SIP table](https://www.amfiindia.com/mutual-fund) reports **1,088.9 lakh outstanding SIP accounts**, **1,006.47 lakh contributing SIP accounts**, **63.26 lakh newly registered SIPs** and **49.68 lakh discontinued/tenure-completed SIPs** for that month. The unit is SIP **accounts/plans**, not distinct people. Conversion of the first number is `1,088.9 lakh / 100 lakh per crore = 10.889 crore SIP accounts`; this does not yield a first-time Groww investor count. The ending category mixes discontinuation with completed tenure, and AMFI notes the failed-instalment cessation rule. **Class:** industry/third-party administrative report; high confidence for the stated units, none for Groww cohort inference.
- [Groww's Q2 FY26 shareholders' letter, p. 4](https://resources.groww.in/web-assets/media-library/2025/11/Q2%20FY26%20Shareholders%27%20Letter.pdf) says **36% of its new platform users** were MF-SIP-first. Denominator is newly joining Groww users that quarter, not first-ever MF investors, not people whose first instalment succeeded, and not a 12-month retained cohort. **Class:** company-reported; high confidence in the reported definition, no conversion to the brief's NSM.
- The brief's NSM still needs a Groww person-level entry cohort, first successful SIP date, and successful instalment events in the last 30 days before each person's 12-month checkpoint. Public account or money totals cannot supply these. **Class:** inference from metric definition; needs Groww data owner.

## Small competitor capability check

- [Groww's skip instructions](https://groww.in/help/mutual-funds/mf-sip/how-to-skip-an-sip-instalment--79) describe a skip control, a two-day cutoff that may defer its effect, and automatic cancellation after three successive skipped/missed instalments. [Groww's missed-instalment instructions](https://groww.in/help/mutual-funds/mf-sip/how-do-i-pay-for-a-missed-installment-3) describe a separate one-time purchase and, for insufficient balance, a conditional Pay Now option lasting two working days. These are **company-documented capabilities and rules**, not observed user comprehension, task success, or recovery rate.
- [Zerodha Coin's MF-SIP pause/resume instructions](https://support.zerodha.com/category/mutual-funds/features-on-coin/systematic-investment-plan/articles/how-do-i-pause-my-sip) document pause and resume for Zerodha SIP orders before a T-1, 5:30 p.m. cutoff; the same page says AMC SIPs cannot be paused. This route distinction matters. **Class:** company-documented capability; not a Groww or first-year investor outcome.
- [INDmoney's MF-SIP page](https://www.indmoney.com/mutual-funds/sip-in-mutual-funds) says investors can edit amount/date, pause or stop future instalments, view upcoming commitments in a SIP Centre, and track returns/XIRR. [Its daily MF-SIP explanation](https://www.indmoney.com/blog/mutual-funds/daily-sips-in-mutual-funds-feature) describes advance notifications and a separate lump-sum purchase after a missed daily instalment. **Class:** company claim, partly marketing; it does not show that every SIP route behaves alike or that users find or complete the control.

**PM interpretation:** Basic adjustment, pause and catch-up capabilities already exist in the market, including at Groww. The current evidence therefore cannot justify a feature simply called “add pause,” “show upcoming SIPs,” or “allow catch-up.” A researchable gap would be whether a *specific first-year person, route and circumstance* can find the right action, understand its effect on the next instalment, and verify the outcome. That remains a question, not a chosen problem or proposed solution. A rival explanation is that the control works fine and the change is an informed, legitimate pause or an external constraint Groww cannot remove.

## Longer-tenure account and expert lane

- In one [TradingQ&A MF-SIP thread from March 2018](https://tradingqna.com/t/reliance-small-cap-not-to-accept-lump-sum-investments/36083), a participant stated that the plan had run for about two years and expressed concern that it could not continue after a scheme-level restriction. A platform representative explained a then-current Coin order-route interaction; the thread does **not** verify that this person redeemed units, that a later SIP succeeded, or that the same rule applies in 2026. The initiating problem involved an AMC restriction and Coin execution route, showing why actor and route must be coded separately from an investor's intent. **Class:** one historical public-user thread with company reply; adjacent/experienced, not a Groww first-year episode or prevalence estimate. Treat the whole thread as one dependent discussion, not 27 independent cases. Link and paraphrase only; [TradingQ&A terms](https://tradingqna.com/tos) license contributions under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0.
- [Value Research's September 2025 editorial story](https://shop.valueresearchonline.com/stories/226180/i-stop-sips-4-reasons-it-makes-sense/) presents an illustrative investor and discusses income disruption, changing goals and other reasons for pausing. It supplies **hypotheses and neutral interview prompts**, not an authenticated first-hand account or a count of investors. Its policy preference for continuing does not override the brief's legitimate-pause guardrail. **Class:** expert/editorial context, not user evidence or investment guidance for this project.

Longer-tenure stories may expose recovery, changed obligations, multiple SIPs, or route issues that a first-year-only search misses. Mark them `adjacent_experienced` and compare their *mechanisms* with target cases. Exclude them from first-year Groww counts and do not assume the same mechanism occurs or is important in the first year.

## What this pass changed, and what remains unproven

1. **Changed:** Competitor review is a necessary **alternative check** before any feature decision. The small official-source comparison demonstrates overlapping adjustment capabilities and different route/cutoff rules. It does not demonstrate a solved retention problem.
2. **Changed:** A long-tenure contrast lane is useful for finding possible mechanisms; it cannot expand the target cohort or answer the NSM.
3. **Still unknown:** How many first-time MF investors start their first SIP on Groww, their 12-month active percentage, the distribution of interruption reasons, and any causal effect of a control. Public totals cannot answer them.
4. **Still unknown:** Whether first-year Groww users encounter a control gap, a comprehension gap, a payment/AMC constraint, or simply an informed decision to pause. The inspected public thread is old and adjacent; the editorial story is not user testimony.

## Next source pass and decision rule

Collect permitted, attributable **investor episodes** from public sources with search terms for ordinary continuation, missed debit, deliberate pause, reduction, recovery and multiple-SIP situations. For each, code stated platform, first-time status, months since first SIP, intent, initiating event, actor/route, action, final state and unknowns. Keep `direct_target`, `adjacent_experienced`, `adjacent_platform`, `unknown_fit`, and `editorial_context` separate. Deduplicate by original thread and avoid counting a quote or repost twice. The nine survey responses remain a secondary triangulation lane.

Compare a candidate problem against the documented Groww and competitor path **only after** finding a target-fit episode with a stated consequence. If public accounts remain adjacent or fragmentary, the engine should show that coverage gap instead of declaring a cause or an opportunity's reach. The next pass should record search date, query, candidates screened, accepted items and exclusions; today's purposive check is not a measured corpus.
