# First-year SIP episode screen — 4 November 2026

## PM decision and method

**Decision served:** Can public web accounts, rather than the small survey, provide source-linked first-year Groww mutual-fund SIP sequences strong enough to identify a candidate moment or reason? The desired observation is a person's stated starting purpose and expectation, a later event, intended next contribution, action taken, and the actual contribution outcome. This screen does **not** select a segment, cause, feature, or estimate prevalence.

This pass used **20 targeted search queries** in five four-query batches: first Groww SIP and failed/first-month language; first-SIP/continuation/restart language; deliberate pause and first-year language; Team-BHP/forum alternatives; and published reader Q&A/first-SIP forum language. Two side queries checked Team-BHP source terms and one secondary editorial lead. Search results were candidate leads only. **Nine original pages** were inspected manually with web and/or the visible native browser. No automatic crawler, raw-text corpus export or LLM analysis was run. Counts below are of inspected **pages**, not investors in the market.

## Source decisions from the nine inspected pages

1. [Personal blog, December 2021](https://blog.tuhinkarmakar.me/2021/12/my-financial-journey.html) — **admit as one reported first-time Groww starting account, partial episode**. The author says the first investing step was a Groww MF SIP in 2018, influenced by a colleague and an accessible small starting amount. The author later describes a sharp fall in the holding's displayed value and says they held on. **What this supports:** a stated starting influence, expectation and reaction to a difficult early period. **What it does not support:** successful first or month-12 instalment, whether the SIP continued without a gap, the app screen seen, or a causal protective factor. “Held the investment” is not the same event as “paid the next SIP.” Older retrospective self-report; transaction history not independently checked. No names or precise amounts are reproduced here.
2. [TradingQ&A first-SIP thread, January 2026](https://tradingqna.com/t/sip-cancel-on-mf/190338) — **admit as one first-SIP episode in an adjacent platform lane**. The author reports paying to start a first MF SIP, not understanding when the next instalment would occur, then cancelling the initial buy order. A platform reply says a refund should follow. **What this supports:** uncertainty about next-schedule meaning can coexist with a completed payment and a reported cancellation action. **What it does not support:** refund completion, first-ever MF-investor status, later SIP status, Groww behaviour, or a month-12 outcome. The initiating event is schedule uncertainty; final state remains unknown. Treat the author and reply as one dependent thread, not two investors. [Forum terms](https://tradingqna.com/tos) allow CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 user contributions; this note links and paraphrases only.

**Arithmetic of this screen:** `9 inspected originals = 1 partial Groww first-time starting account + 1 adjacent first-SIP event + 1 experienced reported interview + 2 provisional claimed stories + 3 editorial/second-hand pages + 1 other PM project`. These are **source-fit dispositions**, not independent-investor incidence. Of the nine, **zero** shows a verified Groww first-time investor with a successful instalment in the 30 days before the 12-month checkpoint. That is a coverage result for nine selected pages, not evidence that continuation does not occur.

## What we learned and what could overturn it

- **Observed in a public first-person account:** an investor may describe maintaining a holding during a difficult period without saying whether scheduled instalments continued. The engine must keep `holding_status` separate from `instalment_status` and let the latter be `unknown`.
- **Observed in an adjacent first-SIP thread:** confusion about the next due date can precede a reported cancellation of an initial order, but the platform and final refund/SIP state differ or remain unconfirmed. This is a candidate question for Groww sources, not a diagnosed Groww problem.
- **Source-quality signal:** first-person SEO articles and an external PM portfolio can sound like research. Admission needs a source-role/provenance label; claimed user testimony is not automatically a verified participant or a measured retention event.
- **Rival explanation:** Early uncertainty or a falling portfolio may be emotionally salient while the SIP continues without failure. The personal blog is compatible with that explanation, as well as with a missed contribution; it cannot separate them.

## Next change in method

Repeating broad web searches is now yielding mostly Reddit results (not admitted under the current [Reddit Responsible Builder Policy](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/42728983564564-Responsible-Builder-Policy)), advice, or retrospective claims without outcomes. Change to a source-specific intake: manually screen recent public MF-SIP reviews and permitted forum/reader Q&A for **stated action and ending**; seek ordinary continuation and recovery as well as problems. Store only link, date, de-identified paraphrase, role and uncertainty. Check each source's current terms before batch collection or model processing. The nine survey responses remain separately labelled secondary evidence.

Decision gate after that intake: if enough target-fit episodes state next-contribution outcomes, compare competing explanations. If not, present a transparent coverage gap; neither the discovery engine nor the case study may claim the NSM rate, a common drop-off moment, or a validated cause from these public pages.
