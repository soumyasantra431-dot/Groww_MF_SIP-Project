## 5 November addendum: cross-platform MF/SIP research supplied by the Fellow

The Fellow supplied a research memo titled *MF/SIP problems across platforms, then Groww — Phase 5 research*. It is useful **context and a candidate-comparison input**, not an accepted engine record, a blind human label, or a model-quality result. The memo's assertion that 28 pages were checked is its own method report, not a second independent audit. The URLs are pages, not investors or corpus rows. The three Reddit pages remain historical manual leads with unresolved model-processing permission.

The memo supports keeping three questions distinct: (1) did the investor intend the scheduled SIP payment, (2) what state was observed at mandate, bank debit, order and unit-allotment steps, and (3) did the following **scheduled** instalment succeed? A later one-time payment and a continuing holding must not silently answer question 3. [Groww's skip help](https://groww.in/help/mutual-funds/mf-sip/how-to-skip-an-sip-instalment--79) documents automatic cancellation after three successive skipped or missed instalments; its [missed-instalment help](https://groww.in/help/mutual-funds/order/why-did-my-installment-fail--25) documents a manual “Pay one-time” option. These are **company-reported capabilities**, not observed successful recovery or a measured first-year Groww problem rate. The [SEBI Investor Survey 2025, Tables 9.2–9.3 (printed pages 58 and 60)](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/commondocs/jan-2026/Investor%20Survey%202025%20Main%20Report.pdf) verifies the MF/ETF response base of 13,862 and selected overlapping challenge percentages; its population cannot be substituted for first-time Groww SIP investors.

The memo's first research-priority candidate is *understanding and recovering from an interrupted intended payment*. Keep that as an **inference for the next comparison**, not as the selected segment, root cause or feature. The strongest rivals are an expected settlement delay, a one-time purchase that did not repair the scheduled instruction, a bank/UPI/mandate problem outside Groww, and an investor's informed pause. Other live candidates are return interpretation, amount/date fit, and understanding control or holding-format consequences. Five competitor help flows show available controls, not that any user found or benefited from them. No candidate has a defensible reach estimate or demonstrated month-12 effect.
