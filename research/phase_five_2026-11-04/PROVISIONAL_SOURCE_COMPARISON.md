## Three candidate explanations to test, without ranking

### A. Meaning of the next payment is unclear

**Reported event:** In a [January 2026 first-SIP post](https://tradingqna.com/t/sip-cancel-on-mf/190338/1), a person says they paid for an initial MF SIP, did not understand when the next instalment would occur, and cancelled the **initial buy order**. They asked when a refund would arrive. **Evidence class:** public user account. **Proposed initiating tag:** first instalment; exit form unknown. The original does not establish that the SIP instruction itself was cancelled, that the refund arrived, that this was the person's first-ever MF investment, or which platform they used. A platform reply describes the schedule and predicts a refund; that reply is not the user's final outcome.

**Possible first-year Groww metric pathway — inference:** if uncertainty about the next scheduled contribution causes a person to cancel the SIP rather than only an initial order, it could interrupt the month-12 qualifying payment. That causal chain is **not observed here**. **Rival:** the person may have recreated the order or retained the SIP after clarification. **Next test:** obtain a first-year Groww account or observed task that separates initial order, SIP schedule and later successful instalment.

### B. A payment route fails, then the person works around it

**Reported event:** In a [December 2023 Coin thread](https://tradingqna.com/t/peculiar-payment-problem-in-coin/156840/1), the poster says a UPI bank-choice mismatch blocked a SIP payment attempt. In their [March 2024 follow-up](https://tradingqna.com/t/peculiar-payment-problem-in-coin/156840/9), the same poster says they changed UPI setup and current payments worked. This is **one topic root and one reported sequence**, not two independent complaints. **Evidence class:** public user account. **Proposed initiating tag:** node unknown, involuntary interruption; the later post is a recovery proposal, while recurring SIP continuation remains unknown. The writer also says they had made many previous SIP payments this way, so the account cannot be used as a first-time investor example. A changed UPI setup followed by success establishes sequence, not which actor caused the original fault or that a month-12 instalment succeeded.

**Possible Groww metric pathway — inference:** a recoverable payment-route block may create a temporary gap that could be repaired if its cause and next step are understandable. **Rival:** the fault may sit with a bank, UPI app or payment rail rather than the investment app; the fix might require an external app change. **Next test:** a Groww first-year debit-failure case with the bank/rail status, user action and the next actual instalment outcome.

### C. Repeated status or support uncertainty could weaken trust

**Reported events, not a single person:** One [Coin poster in January 2023](https://tradingqna.com/t/discontinuation-of-pooling-for-mutual-funds-and-how-it-affects-coin/120947/924) reported a debit without prompt unit allocation and another scheduled debit not yet made. Their [later update](https://tradingqna.com/t/discontinuation-of-pooling-for-mutual-funds-and-how-it-affects-coin/120947/926) says the second debit subsequently occurred. A **different** poster in the [same discussion](https://tradingqna.com/t/discontinuation-of-pooling-for-mutual-funds-and-how-it-affects-coin/120947/935) said they paused investments because of concern about future problems. A [separate Coin thread](https://tradingqna.com/t/delay-in-reporting-of-mutual-fund-mandate-debit/160104/1) describes repeated status delays and asks whether manual payment would be safer; it does not report making that switch. **Evidence class:** public user accounts. The platform's reply about banks or rails is an actor attribution, not verified fault for each individual case.

**Possible Groww metric pathway — inference:** uncertainty after a debit could prompt repeated checking, support contact or a pause. **Rival:** a delayed unit display may occur after a successful debit and may never change future SIP payments; the voluntary pause in the topic came from a different person. **Next test:** a linked first-year Groww chronology with debit, exchange/AMC allocation, in-app status, support interaction, and the next contribution.
