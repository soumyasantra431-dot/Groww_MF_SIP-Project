## Source fit and the important rival

The selected originals describe reported actions such as cancelling an initial buy after schedule uncertainty, a payment route failing and later changing, a deliberate pause after seeing other problems, and changed transaction checking after rejected SIPs. These are **adjacent platform accounts**, mostly Coin, with one experienced Paytm Money account. They can test the engine's distinctions between an intended action, payment event and later outcome. They cannot establish a Groww failure mode, a first-ever MF cohort, the month-12 active event, or prevalence. Some are from 2021–2024 and may describe product mechanics that have changed; the 2026 first-SIP account is still on Coin.

A credible rival to any future “the app caused stopping” story is that a person made an informed pause because of an outside constraint, or that a payment issue occurred at a bank/rail while the platform control worked. The current corpus cannot separate those explanations for Groww. We therefore **do not name a target segment, cause, or feature** from these posts.

**Phase 1 exit gate:** passed for source intake and transparent accounting: one permitted local path worked; every new row has a source link, locator and recorded basis; all seven lanes show their true status. **Research sufficiency:** not passed for a Groww-specific opportunity because direct-target coverage is zero.
