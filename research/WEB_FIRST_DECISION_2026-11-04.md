# Web-first discovery decision — 4 November 2026

## Decision and purpose

The user's latest count is **nine survey responses**, to be treated as secondary evidence; this pass did not open or recount them. The primary research route is public web material. The next decision is **which usable source families can supply target-relevant first-year Groww SIP episodes, including ordinary continuation and interruption, with enough sequence detail to inform the nine discovery questions?** This is a source-yield and evidence-fit question before opportunity selection, not a search for proof of a preferred cause.

The earlier suggestion to seek one interruption and one continuer is retained as a **contrast to look for within the corpus**, not as a sufficient research sample. Do not select a segment, month, cause, or feature from two stories.

## Initial web screen completed today

Four focused search batches (four queries each; **16 search queries**, not 16 screened investors) sought Groww/first-SIP interruptions, continuation and recovery, plus forum and published-reader alternatives. Search results were leads only. The visible results contained many stock SIP, Coin, older-investor, generic advice, or simulated/editorial accounts. None of those is automatically a first-year Groww investor episode. This search was not a systematic corpus sample, so it yields no rate or saturation claim.

One [TradingQ&A conversation](https://tradingqna.com/t/peculiar-payment-problem-in-coin/156840) was inspected to its later author update in the native browser. **Public user account:** a Coin user reported a previously used SIP payment route failing, then reported that a different UPI app/account setup with the primary bank worked. The thread does not establish first-ever MF status, first-year tenure, a qualifying Groww instalment, or the technical root cause. It is **adjacent evidence** of reported recovery and the importance of checking the ending, not a target-cohort finding. No username, account data or raw post text is copied into this note.

Source-use screen: [TradingQ&A terms](https://tradingqna.com/tos) say user contributions are under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0; this note uses a link and a short paraphrase, with no full-text republication. [Reddit's Responsible Builder Policy](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/42728983564564-Responsible-Builder-Policy), updated 5 October 2026, says research using Reddit data outside its approved researcher programme violates the policy. Reddit search results encountered today are **not admitted to a new corpus or model-input queue**. A public search result is not permission for collection or AI processing.

## Next bounded research pass

1. Build a source register for Play Store, App Store, TradingQ&A, YouTube comments and other public conversations: access/terms, search method, date, candidate count, admitted count, and why excluded. Reddit stays blocked unless an approved route is established. Company help and industry documents remain context, never user-voice counts.
2. Screen for **mutual fund SIP** relevance and target fit: explicitly first-time MF, first SIP, Groww, and months 0–12 where stated. Keep adjacent and unknown separate. Search ordinary-success, adjustment, recovery and informed-pause language alongside failure terms.
3. For admitted accounts, record only a locator and de-identified paraphrase with stated purpose, expected next contribution, trigger/circumstance, intention, event, in/out-of-app action, ending, and what is unknown. Treat one thread and its replies as a dependency group; do not count repeated mentions as independent investors.
4. Check yield after a small, auditable first batch. If the web supplies mainly short reviews or unknown endings, use them for the questions they can answer, mark questions 2, 4, 7 and 8 weak, and use the nine survey responses only as separately labelled triangulation. Do not pad missing target evidence with mock stories or generic expert advice.

## What would change the decision

If permitted web accounts provide target-fit episodes with stated intention and ending, proceed to structured extraction and contrasting synthesis. If they provide only adjacent/fragmentary reports, the engine can still demonstrate source-bounded classification and honest unknowns, but cannot claim a confirmed first-year cause. Groww cohort incidence, month-12 NSM, silent continuers/drop-offs, and causal effect remain unavailable from this public corpus.
