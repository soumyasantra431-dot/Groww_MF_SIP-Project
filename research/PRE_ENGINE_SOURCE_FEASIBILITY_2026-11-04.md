# Pre-engine source feasibility check — 4 November 2026

## Decision this research serves

**Question:** Before engineering, which named public sources can supply first-year mutual-fund SIP user evidence, and which routes can actually be used? The intended engine must answer the brief's nine questions from traceable accounts, then compare *candidate* opportunities. This pass tests source fit and access. It does not estimate Groww retention, select a segment or cause, create a corpus, or run an AI model.

The [nine-question evidence audit](NINE_QUESTION_EVIDENCE_AUDIT_2026-11-04.md) sets the needed observation: purpose and expectation, a first-year event, intended next contribution, action, and ending. A person holding fund units is not proof that another scheduled instalment succeeded. One investor counts once in the business metric, even if several SIPs exist.

## What was checked

I manually opened the original [Groww Play Store listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nextbillion.groww&hl=en_IN) and [Groww App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/groww-stocks-mutual-fund-ipo/id1404871703?platform=iphone&see-all=reviews) in the visible browser to assess the *kind* of material visible. No review text, usernames, screenshots, raw export, or review-level labels were retained. The landing pages mix stocks, one-time mutual-fund actions, support issues and possible SIP mentions. An apparent mention of continued investing does not establish first-ever mutual-fund status or a successful month-12 SIP instalment. Landing-page presentation is selected by the stores; it is not a representative or SIP-filtered sample. This observation cannot become a rate or claim of typical user behaviour.

Official access documents were inspected rather than assuming an API key could unlock reviews:

- **Play Store:** Google's [Reply to Reviews documentation](https://developers.google.com/android-publisher/reply-to-reviews?hl=en) says the API retrieves reviews for **your app** with authorised access. The [reviews.list reference](https://developers.google.com/android-publisher/api-ref/rest/v3/reviews/list) requires Android Publisher authorisation. A free Google API key held by the Fellow does not grant access to Groww's owner-side review feed. Public-page review collection needs its own permitted method and selection rule; no such batch is approved or executed here.
- **App Store:** Apple's [App Store Connect review API](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appstoreconnectapi/customer-reviews) is for reviews of **your app**. More decisively, [Apple's India Media Services terms, section F](https://www.apple.com/in/legal/internet-services/itunes/in/terms-en.html) prohibit scraping, copying, or measurement/analysis of service content by automated or equivalent manual process, and restrict use of service outputs for improving AI software. **Decision:** do not build an App Store review collector or admit those reviews as a research corpus under this route. This is a source-access decision, not an inference about App Store users.
- **Reddit:** the [Responsible Builder Policy](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/42728983564564-Responsible-Builder-Policy) requires an approved route for Reddit-data research. The [web-first screen](WEB_FIRST_DECISION_2026-11-04.md) did not admit Reddit posts. Reading search leads does not confer collection or model-processing rights.
- **TradingQ&A:** [forum terms](https://tradingqna.com/tos) license user contributions CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. The [first-SIP conversation](https://tradingqna.com/t/sip-cancel-on-mf/190338) supplies one stated sequence: next-date uncertainty and cancellation of an initial order. It concerns Coin, so it remains in an **adjacent platform** lane; its later SIP outcome is unknown. Link and paraphrase only; a thread with replies is one dependency group, not several independent investors. The [first web episode screen](FIRST_WEB_EPISODE_SCREEN_2026-11-04.md) records this and other inspected originals.
- **YouTube comments, public social, and other conversations:** not yet source-screened in this pass. A free API or public page is not by itself permission, relevance, or evidence of first-year Groww SIP outcomes. Record source-specific terms, retrieval method and selection bias before collection. Published expert articles may inform hypotheses and interview prompts but do not count as investor outcomes unless they contain an attributable first-hand account.

## PM conclusion in plain language

The brief names places to *look*, not seven guaranteed live data feeds. A review often reports a complaint but omits the person's first-SIP date, intended next payment and eventual payment result. A longer forum account may explain a sequence, but could concern another platform or an experienced investor. Both can be useful when labelled accurately; neither supplies the Groww cohort denominator or month-12 outcome by itself.

**Source fit by question:** short reviews can flag possible payment/status confusion (question 5) and lead to a follow-up; first-hand narratives can sometimes illuminate starting motive, change, outside-app action and recovery (questions 1–3, 6). Question 4's checking frequency, question 7's quiet drop-offs and question 8's segment differences remain particularly weak in public posts. Question 9 can be a comparison of *candidate* needs only after independent, reviewed episodes and counterexamples are present. Company help pages prove available controls, not that users understand or use them.

## Smallest remaining pre-engine decisions

1. **Source contract:** define a permitted intake route per family and mark unavailable families honestly. Do not substitute invented reviews or mock interviews for user evidence. Synthetic fixtures may test software later, visibly separated from research counts.
2. **Admissibility:** accept only source-linked first-hand or clearly attributed reports relevant to a nine-question field; label `direct Groww first-year`, `adjacent`, or `unknown`. Record stated event and ending separately from inference. Reject personal identifiers before saving.
3. **Counting:** report `screened → eligible user-voice → target-fit → complete episodes → human-reviewed`, with each denominator and reason for exclusion. Count one thread/root account once. Never turn a convenience corpus count into prevalence or the North Star Metric.
4. **Output contract:** each of the nine answers must state the supported observation, source link, contrary or unresolved case, and what remains unknown. Opportunity comparison needs evidence strength and next test, without an automatic winning feature.
5. **Evaluation:** freeze a small real, independently human-labelled set before claiming extraction quality. AI-proposed labels remain proposals until reviewed.

**Stop before engineering:** the next step is a concrete, reviewable engine contract using these decisions, followed by the user's requested check-in before any coding. Corpus intake and fuller question analysis belong to the engine/research workflow after that contract; today's source check is a feasibility gate, not a substitute for the later corpus.
