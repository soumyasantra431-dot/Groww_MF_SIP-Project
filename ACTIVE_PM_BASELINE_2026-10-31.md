## 1. The actual assignment

We are acting as a Growth PM for Groww. The exact north star metric is: **“Increase the percentage of first-time mutual fund investors who still have an active SIP 12 months after starting their first one.”** Active means **at least one successful instalment in the last 30 days**. The submission deadline is **9 November 2026, 3:59 PM IST**.

The brief gives an outcome to improve. It does **not** give a proved user problem, a target segment within the cohort, a reason for stopping, a moment to intervene or a feature to build. Our PM job is to discover a consequential, changeable first-year situation and test the strongest rival explanation before choosing a customer solution.

For a mature, fully defined cohort, the metric is `qualifying unique investors / all eligible unique investors at the month-12 checkpoint × 100`. One person counts once, regardless of SIP count or retries. A smaller successful SIP may still qualify. An app visit, active mandate, holding, attempted debit, one-time purchase or twelve-payment streak is not the defined success event. The exact cohort entry, cross-platform first-time status, transaction finality and observation window remain unsettled; public posts cannot provide the numerator, denominator or baseline. Preserve `N = confirmed active + confirmed inactive + unresolved` rather than converting unresolved to zero or inactive.

## 2. What the first-year journey could contain

Start with intention and expectation, then reconstruct the person's circumstance, initiating event, action and reported ending. Distinguish a payment or mandate interruption, a deliberate pause or reduction, apparent drift, recovery, ordinary continuation and a legitimate changed plan. One stopped SIP does not prove that an investor has no active SIP. A bank debit or separate lump-sum purchase does not prove a successful scheduled instalment. An informed pause may serve the investor even if it does not improve the company metric.

Possible underlying factors belong to three lenses: **investor circumstances**, **system conditions**, and **actor incentives/responsibility**. These are questions, not established causes. The investor, Groww, bank/payment rails, order platform, AMC and registrar can each play a different part. Record whom a writer blames separately from what actually failed. A market move is context or a trigger; “markets fell” is not a sufficient root cause.

## 4. Evidence and decision rules to retain

For every proposed finding: state the PM decision it could change, the missing fact, the method capable of observing that fact, the original source, a rival explanation, the arithmetic unit/denominator and the reversal condition. Separate **company-reported capability**, **third-party context**, **public user self-report**, **PM inference** and **unknown**. Preserve ordinary continuers, recovery and informed pauses as contrasts. Do not turn complaint volume into Groww prevalence or a mock story into an investor account.
