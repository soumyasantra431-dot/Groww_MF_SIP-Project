## 1. Executive verdict and decision

The Phase 5 decision is whether four plausible **first-year Groww SIP problems** can be compared with source-linked behavioural evidence, and whether a varied real-input set can be prepared for a later independent AI check. The North Star remains: “Increase the percentage of first-time mutual fund investors who still have an active SIP 12 months after starting their first one.” Here *active* means at least one successful SIP instalment in the preceding 30 days. The **next scheduled instalment** is a separate diagnostic outcome we seek in episodes; a separate one-time purchase does not prove it. The numerator and denominator are **unique eligible investors**, never posts, SIP plans, orders, retry attempts or rupees. Neither can be estimated here.

- **Closed:** The four specified TradingQ&A readings were reopened. An initial Coin order cancellation, a Coin payment-route recovery, a Coin trust-driven investment pause and a mistaken deletion of one SIP are distinct events. The payment-route recovery does not establish the next scheduled instalment. The mistaken deletion does not redeem existing holdings. Current Groww help documents Pay one-time, skip, edit, bank/AutoPay change, processing-delay and support routes. [Forum terms](https://tradingqna.com/tos) and [Groww terms](https://groww.in/pages/terms-and-conditions-groww) were rechecked before adding any source interpretation.
- **Partly closed:** Adjacent forum accounts distinguish a failed mandate, delayed allocation, a deliberate trust pause, a manual payment workaround and a platform-switch consideration. One Coin account reports the **same scheduled mandate failed in two consecutive months**; this is a known *next scheduled failure* in an adjacent platform, not a Groww first-year case. The source-side evaluation candidate set can be frozen at 20 real forum post locators, subject to privacy and processor review. These are not 20 independent investors.
- **Open:** `0` strict-cohort Groww accounts with a known later scheduled-instalment outcome; `0` comparable first-year Groww continuer pairs; no measured reach, month-12 effect or causal separation; no researcher-approved answer to any of the nine questions; no independent real-item AI quality result. ## 2. PM inference chain and stop decision

**Observation → interpretation → need/friction → possible metric path → rival → confidence/limit:**

1. This supports a **route-specific recovery episode**. A person may need to know which step failed and which alternate route is safe. A missed scheduled debit could reduce last-30-day active status if the next scheduled contribution also fails. **Rival:** the problem may sit with bank/UPI routing rather than platform comprehension; the later successful payments may be manual and are not a dated next scheduled debit. **Confidence:** high in the reported sequence, low in actor/root cause and North Star linkage.

This supports a **control-meaning/date comprehension question**, not a proved first-year continuation failure. A person may need to distinguish initial purchase, SIP schedule and refund. **Rival:** this may have been a reversible initial order choice, and the SIP schedule might have remained intact. The next scheduled payment is unknown. **Confidence:** high in reported action, low in eventual consequence.
3. [E10](https://tradingqna.com/t/best-alternative-to-zerodha-coin/136294/7) reports that two of several Paytm Money SIPs were rejected, after which the writer began checking transactions more closely. This is **failure preceding checking**, a counterexample to assuming checking caused stopping. A person may need intelligible reconciliation and support. **Rival:** underlying bank/exchange issue; no later SIP stop is stated. **Confidence:** moderate in the self-report, low in any causal or Groww inference.
4. [E03](https://tradingqna.com/t/discontinuation-of-pooling-for-mutual-funds-and-how-it-affects-coin/120947/505) reported pausing Coin investments despite more than ten transactions without trouble after seeing others' trouble; the [same writer later reported a trust-based Coin pause](https://tradingqna.com/t/discontinuation-of-pooling-for-mutual-funds-and-how-it-affects-coin/120947/935). This shows **risk perception can affect action without a personal failed payment**. **Rival:** an informed, sensible decision during system uncertainty, not a problem to reverse. Whether any scheduled SIP stopped is unknown. **Confidence:** high in stated pause, low in SIP/metric consequence. These two posts are **one writer**, not two independent investors.

**Decision note:** Keep payment-state understanding/recovery as a *research candidate*, not the winner. Its closest rival is an actual bank, UPI, mandate, scheme or settlement failure for which clearer UI alone would not prevent missed payments. The smallest test is a target-fit first-year Groww event with the due date, debit/order/allotment timeline and next scheduled payment outcome, contrasted with a continuer through a comparable event. Change research priority if that evidence shows correct comprehension and normal continuation, or if expectation/affordability/control evidence yields a clearer direct outcome chain. Stop collecting another generic failed-payment complaint that cannot distinguish these explanations.
