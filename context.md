# Evidence context for the Groww SIP discovery engine

**As of:** 2 November 2026. **Purpose:** record only what changes the engine's source selection, coding, answers, evaluation or delivery. This is a build input, not a claim that the project has found the winning user problem. Read with [the problem statement](problem%20statement.md), [PM charter](PROJECT_CHARTER.md) and [build handoff](BUILD_HANDOFF_2026-11-02.md).

Links into `research/` refer to the **local private research folder**, which is Git ignored. If these documents are later shared through GitHub, publish a separately reviewed, privacy-safe source index or retain the limits and counts written here; do not expose raw research files merely to make links work.

## 1. What the research was trying to decide

The public research asked: **which parts of a first-year SIP journey and which kinds of reported account would the engine need to distinguish before a PM can compare possible continuation problems?** This is narrower than estimating Groww's actual month-twelve retention or identifying a dominant cause. Company and industry sources map capability, actors and measurement units. Public accounts reveal reported sequences, wording, confusion, actions and counterexamples. Neither substitutes for Groww event data or a representative sample.

The PM method used throughout is: **decision → consequential unknown → suitable source/method → source-backed observation → rival explanation → updated next step/reversal condition**. All later engine output must follow the same logic. Evidence labels are **company-reported**, **third-party original**, **public user account**, **inference** or **unknown**. A model proposal and a reviewed fact are different states.

## 2. Relevant findings and their limits

### Metric and population context

- **Company-reported / third-party, strong for definitions, not cohort size.** Groww disclosures and industry sources use different units: platform transacting users, MF investors, SIP accounts, AUM and contributions. The public [population and journey research](research/population_journey_2026-10-30/findings.md) contains **80 claim records** reconciled as `14 population + 36 journey + 6 motivation + 6 public-account + 18 calendar = 80`. It also has **41 activity-map rows**, one of which is an analytical checkpoint, and **18 dated context records**. These are research records, **not** 80 source roots, 41 observed user actions or 18 causal triggers.
- **Unknown.** Public disclosures do not provide the eligible first-ever-MF/first-SIP investor-level denominator or successful-instalment month-twelve numerator. The engine must not calculate Groww's NSM from industry SIP accounts, app reviews or money values. It can identify candidate metric nodes, then request a Groww data-owner cut: cohort by first-SIP start, qualifying success, month-1–12 survival, failed-payment reasons, adjustments and recovery, with unique investors and unknown observation status.
- **Company-reported context, not user evidence.** MF is a relationship/acquisition product in Groww's reporting; a past Q2 FY27 SIP flow of ₹14,347 crore is a money value, not a person count or incremental NSM value. Groww's own retention definition differs from this assignment's recent-success definition. Relevant route codes are **direct, regular (including MF Prime possibility), mixed and unknown**. Neither the commercial value of this NSM change nor first-time users' route distribution is measured publicly. See [business and project audit](research/project_audit_2026-10-31/AUDIT.md).

### Journey and actor context

- **Company-reported capability, not observed behaviour.** Official material describes discovery, KYC, fund/amount/date choice, mandate/AutoPay, first and later instalments, returns/status views, skip/edit, conditional pay-now versus a separate one-time purchase, step-up, statements, help, learning, GR-1 and MF Prime. Availability depends on route, rollout or mandate conditions, and some screens need a live-app test. The [activity map](research/population_journey_2026-10-30/journey_activity_map.csv) is a list of test and coding candidates, not proof that a given investor saw a control or that it worked. GR-1 launch is scope context, not evidence of SIP payment repair. BER display change is context, not proof that costs fell or confusion rose.
- **Research synthesis / inference.** Capability, investor intention, payment state and user outcome require separate fields. Actor location can differ from investor blame: investor, platform, bank, AutoPay/payment rails, exchange/order platform, AMC and registrar may each be involved. Code **what the author attributes**, and keep actual technical responsibility unknown without transaction evidence. A successful separate purchase does not prove recovery of the scheduled SIP.
- **Calendar context, not causation.** The [18-record calendar](research/population_journey_2026-10-30/trigger_calendar.csv) helps interpret a source's date, market/rule/product environment and potential exposure. It cannot establish that an event caused an individual's action. Preserve publication date, cohort-relative month and first-SIP date separately; often the latter is unknown.

### Public voice: what was observed

- **Public user accounts, self-report only.** The [comparison inventory](research/explanation_comparison_2026-10-30/findings.md) contains **22 source-account records across 21 conversation roots**: `12 P + 2 P + 8 E = 22`. Platform attribution reconciles as `11 Groww-stated + 2 Coin-adjacent + 9 platform-unknown = 22`. These are not 22 confirmed first-time Groww MF investors. All **22 independently verified month-twelve outcomes are unknown**. The [eight sequence records](research/behaviour_sequences_2026-10-30/findings.md) overlap that inventory and must not be added as eight new people or roots.
- **Observed reported contrast, not a measured cause distribution.** A missed scheduled instalment can coexist with later catch-up intent and a confusing failed status. Checking can reflect excitement or concern and need not imply exit. App removal can be an attention-management attempt. A person may ask about a control before taking action. Another may increase contributions despite disappointment; a legitimate financial break may be intentional. These examples demand fields for expectation, trigger, intention, action, status, workaround and reported ending, plus explicit negation/hypothetical handling.
- **Inference with rival explanations.** Four comparison tracks remain open: payment/execution trouble; uncertainty about status/control meaning; expectations versus observation; and changed circumstances/informed choice. A visible complaint could instead be a precautionary question, a one-time order rather than SIP recovery, or an unresolved processing event. Complaint frequency cannot rank these explanations in the Groww cohort. The most useful follow-up asks **what the person wanted the next contribution to do and what actually happened next**.
- **Unknown and likely weak.** Public text rarely states a reliable checking frequency (brief Q4), stable month-by-month chronology (Q2), income stability/funding/influence (Q8), silent continuation (Q7) or independently verified payment outcome. Keep unanswered cells visible. Do not fill them from stereotypes, company totals, inferred age, subreddit name or a reviewer's intuition.

## 3. Input lanes, rights and actual volume

**User-voice research lane:** source-linked, de-identified real public accounts with a recorded source-use basis. **Consented first-hand lane:** seven existing written survey responses are secondary triangulation, not public corpus or interviews; the Fellow has reported permission for selecting any three for de-identified AI analysis, while individual consent receipts/dates are not independently verified here. **Context lane:** company/regulator/industry/expert documents. **Engineering lane:** synthetic fixtures. Keep these four lanes separate in counts and UI. The genuine low-detail survey submission remains a real response, even if it supports few fields.

The local engine has **20 historical inputs, zero coded real results, zero completed nine-question narrative answers and zero researcher-named candidate problems** at this checkpoint, per [handoff](BUILD_HANDOFF_2026-11-02.md). The ten-input frozen pilot is a separate measurement pack, not an additional analysed corpus. Twelve synthetic fixture cases test code/transport and count as **zero investor evidence**. The 130-label mapping for ten items is **post-AI development reference**, not independent blind human gold. Therefore no engine accuracy, seven-family coverage, segment reach or opportunity ranking can yet be claimed.

The seven brief families are **accepted input categories**, not seven operational connectors. The current [source route decision](research/SOURCE_ROUTE_DECISION_2026-11-02.md) and [terms register](research/ENGINE_V1_TERMS_REGISTER.json) say:

- **TradingQ&A:** bounded, noncommercial, local academic-use/model-processing interpretation recorded; public raw-text/expressive-paraphrase redistribution held. Other forums and reader Q&A need their own checks.
- **Play/App Store:** current developer review APIs do not grant access to Groww's owner reviews; no owner export is available. A cleared alternate route is required.
- **Reddit:** approved research-use route unresolved; existing seed excerpts remain local and out of model calls. Manual copying is not a bypass.
- **YouTube:** official comment API exists; the source-specific collection/derived-use decision remains unresolved. No active collector use is claimed.
- **Social/other:** named platform and original-specific source/use review needed.

The engine must report `candidate → screened → admitted → de-identified → deduplicated → coded → reviewed → answer-eligible` by source family, including exclusions and why. A spreadsheet of fictional stories would not repair a missing real corpus. A link without text has not been analysed.

## 4. Evaluation and engineering facts that constrain the design

The current local stack is React 19 + TypeScript + Vite on `127.0.0.1:4317`, Node 24 + built-in SQLite, Groq provider adapter + Zod, with ignored `.local` data and `.dev.vars` key. Git is initialized but has **no commit or remote**. The eventual public design is a **read-only derived snapshot** and, only after validation, a guarded live paste classifier; no raw corpus or admin route is published. See [architecture decision](research/ENGINE_ARCHITECTURE_LOCK_2026-11-02.md).

The lean extraction contract is ten material fields plus `metric_node` and `exit_form`, each `supported | unknown | not_applicable` with an original-text anchor. The larger 13-bundle/factor extraction is a stretch for **3–5 contrasting real narratives** once lean extraction works. Model output is only a proposal; code checks structure/counts and a researcher checks meaning. The old full-packet batch/paste routes are held and the lean pilot is not integrated into them.

The [lean pilot report](research/pilot_smoke_2026-11-01/LEAN_PILOT_REVIEW_2026-11-02.md) records **six** Groq Free development attempts overall: two HTTP 413, one HTTP 200 rejected locally for invalid structure, three synthetic strict-schema HTTP 400 (`json_validate_failed`). **Zero validated extraction**, **zero real frozen measurement calls**, **USD 0 recorded spend**. Five failed-attempt token reservations remain unresolved, not measured charges. Engineering tests and the frontend build passed previously, but do not validate model truthfulness. The pre-registered ten-item smoke thresholds are zero severe errors, at least 70% recall on supported fields, at least 90% preservation of unknowns, at most one processing failure in ten, and exclusion/flagging of non-episodes. The existing human gold is incomplete, so even a structural pass alone would not yield an independent accuracy claim.

**Decision checkpoint:** if lean extraction is not validated by **3 November 2026, 4 PM IST**, disclose the AI path as unvalidated and use a visibly labelled, manually reviewed evidence workspace with deterministic counts. Do not describe it as completed AI analysis or hide the brief's engine shortfall. No paid fallback is authorised or required by this plan.

## 5. What this context makes the engine do next

1. Build a **source-cleared, real corpus** with ordinary continuation, recovery, informed pause and unwanted interruption, recording selection and missingness. The earlier target of 280–350 triage items and about 40 deep episodes is aspirational; the earlier first gate was 100 real items from at least three families with 20 trigger/action narratives. Report actual counts and shortfall; do not pad.
2. Make the **lean extraction reliable on synthetic checks**, then run the frozen ten-item smoke according to the registered rules and adjudicate source meaning. Treat reuse of post-AI labels as development, not blind evaluation.
3. Code real accounts with distinct-root counting, stated factors/protective cues where present, initiating node and exit form plus final state. Separate reported reason, supported situation and labelled driver hypothesis.
4. Compose nine cited, bounded answers **after** reviewed real extraction. Compare researcher-named candidate situations without a score or automatic winner. Triangulate separately with the written survey and record agreements, challenges and unknowns.
5. Hand the strongest still-open question to focused primary research and, if accessible, a Groww data owner. Do not choose the customer problem merely because the engine produced a polished paragraph.
