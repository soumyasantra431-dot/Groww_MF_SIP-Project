# Copy-ready first-year SIP research exchange

**7 November 2026.** This is a short, two-way written interview protocol for the Fellow to send to people directly. It is **not** a completed interview, a simulated participant, a representative survey or a substitute for asking follow-up questions. Use the longer [neutral guide](PART2_3_OPPORTUNITY_QUANTIFICATION_AND_INTERVIEW_GUIDE_2026-11-07.md) for live conversations. Record a de-identified ID such as R01; keep any ID-to-person key outside project materials. Do not forward names, contacts, workplaces, exact balances, PAN, bank messages, account numbers or screenshots. Participation and AI-assisted analysis require the person's affirmative agreement.

## Message 1 — invite and screen

> I am doing an academic project about people's first year using a mutual-fund SIP on Groww. I want to understand ordinary months and any changes, including cases where reducing or stopping was the right choice. Would you answer a few questions in writing and one or two follow-ups? I will use de-identified notes, and may use AI tools to help analyse those notes. Please reply YES only if you agree to both the interview and that de-identified AI analysis. You can skip any question or stop at any time. Please do not send screenshots, account details, PAN, exact balances or bank messages.
>
> Before starting: (1) Had you invested in **any mutual fund, on any platform**, before your first Groww mutual-fund SIP? Yes / No / Unsure. (2) Was your **first mutual-fund SIP** started on Groww? Yes / No / Unsure. (3) Roughly which month and year did it start? A range is fine. (4) Have you had any SIP contribution in the past month? Yes / No / Unsure / Prefer not to say. Please do not send an amount.

Only a person who answers **No** to (1) and **Yes** to (2) is explicitly eligible for the brief's first-time-MF, first-Groww-SIP cohort; keep unsure as unknown, not eligible by assumption. Question (4) is a self-report, not verification of a *successful* instalment. People at 12–18 months since start can describe a completed first year; newer starters help test early understanding but cannot provide their month-12 ending. Do not choose only people who report a failed payment.

## Message 2 — six open prompts to an eligible or clearly labelled adjacent person

> Thanks. Please answer in your own words; short answers are fine. Avoid exact amounts and identifying details.
>
> 1. What were you hoping a recurring SIP would help you do when you started? What did you expect to happen after the first payment? Who or what influenced you, if anyone?
> 2. Tell me the story from your first Groww SIP to now. What was routine, and what changed? Please describe one ordinary month as well as any memorable one.
> 3. For the month you remember best, what did you want the **next scheduled contribution** to do? What happened first, what did Groww or another service show, and what did you think it meant?
> 4. What did you do next—inside Groww or elsewhere—and what result did you see? What happened at the **following scheduled date**, as far as you know? If you had more than one SIP, did another one continue?
> 5. Think of your last **two actual times** checking your SIP or mutual fund. What prompted each check, what did you look at, and what did you do with what you learned? If there were no two checks, say that.
> 6. Did the result match what you wanted at that time? What, if anything, took extra effort or remained unclear? Was any choice to skip, reduce or stop intentional and right for you?

## One tailored follow-up after each reply

The researcher reads the answer first and asks **one or two neutral questions about the biggest missing link**, without presenting a theory. Examples: “You mentioned ‘pending’; what did you think it meant then, and what later showed the final result?”; “You said you stopped one SIP; was that your only SIP?”; “You said the payment failed; was a mandate being set up, a scheduled debit due, or a separate purchase being attempted?”; “You mention a changed income pattern; did you intend to keep the same amount, change it or stop for now?” Do not infer a bank cause from an app message, or a month-12 outcome from an active label.

After each actual exchange, code separately: **eligibility**, months since start, stated goal, income pattern/source only if volunteered, initiating event, intent, action, app/bank signal, interpretation, workaround, next scheduled result, other SIP, stated consequence, informed choice, rival explanation and unknowns. Report `screened → eligible → completed exchange → reconstructable episode → known next-due result`, with counts and denominators. Five or six completed two-way exchanges satisfy the intended qualitative sample **only if they belong to the chosen target segment**; a one-off survey response is not an interview. Preserve verbatim consent privately and publish only de-identified summaries.
