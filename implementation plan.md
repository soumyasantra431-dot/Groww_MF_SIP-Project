## 0. A simple explanation of the build

We will first put **real, permitted accounts** into a private research workspace. The AI will propose what each account says about a SIP event; software will check that the proposal is well formed and count distinct sources; a reviewer will check it against the original. Only then will the tool write plain-English answers to the nine assignment questions and compare possible problems. The public demo will show **derived, reviewed evidence** and its source links. The original text and key stay on this computer. If the AI keeps failing, we will show a clearly labelled manual evidence path and say exactly which AI requirement is still unmet.

## Phase 4 — Compute evidence, write nine answers, compare problems

**Dependency:** source-reviewed real records, from AI or labelled manual fallback. **Purpose:** turn accounts into questions the PM can act on. **Work:** deduplicate records and group dependent replies; deterministically compute the source funnel, cohort-fit mix, source-family mix, node × exit form, unknowns and question coverage. Preserve final state, action status, stated reason, supported situation, driver hypothesis, protective cues, counterexample and rival. Make **nine** question pages from reviewed, cited claims and computed counts only. For Q4, say checking frequency is unknown unless sources actually state it; for Q2, require same-person chronology; for Q7, do not treat invisible silent continuers as observed.

**Gate 4:** each answer passes a reviewer check: every factual clause has a source ID; count/denominator/unit/formula and exclusions agree with the ledger; direct versus adjacent versus unknown cohort fit is visible; a counterexample/rival and next test are present. Show “insufficient evidence” where necessary. A model may draft prose from approved evidence, but may not create counts, sources or unsupported causal statements.

**Insight check:** before accepting a pattern, name the research objective, the working hypothesis and its strongest alternative; check whether the chosen source can observe the claim; then separate reported event, interpretation and PM implication. A survey or public post is a self-report. A source-linked count describes this collected corpus, not observed Groww behaviour or population incidence. If this check fails, narrow the wording or change the research method.

**Opportunity view:** a researcher enters candidate problem statements, not reason codes. Compare evidence strength, corpus reach proxy, user consequence, plausible NSM link, constraints, rival and next test. No numerical winner or automatic segment selection. Survey responses may agree or challenge a candidate in a separate triangulation export; seven convenience responses do not establish prevalence. **Output:** evaluator-readable, grounded engine answers and comparison. If there are zero justified candidates, display that honestly.

## Milestone report format and stop rules

At the end of each phase report: **what exists; real items/roots by lane; coded, reviewed and excluded counts; actual model calls/tokens/latency/spend; what is unrun; one PM decision and its rival; next gate/reversal condition**. State the source and denominator for every number. Run the least additional test that resolves a material risk; stop optional testing after the gate is clear.

Stop the affected source or model route if permission is absent, PII is present, a source's terms conflict, or a model claim exceeds its source. Stop publication if private/raw material appears in a staged artifact. If a source cannot answer a question, change method rather than manufacturing a larger dataset. **No paid call, no fabricated participant, no invented success metric.**
