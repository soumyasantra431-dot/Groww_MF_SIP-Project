## Definition of complete

An evaluator can open the public link and, in about two minutes, understand the first-year SIP question, see what each of the seven named source families actually contributed, inspect one original behind a claim, read a bounded result or explicit evidence gap for all nine questions, compare unranked research situations with credible rivals, and see how the AI was evaluated. A pasted public item gets an AI proposal **only if** the frozen quality gate passes; otherwise the tester explains the hold and offers a genuine source walkthrough. No raw corpus, survey identifiers, key, or unapproved source material is published.

**Completeness is not nine affirmative findings.** “Checking frequency unknown in this public corpus” is a complete and honest Q4 result. Source links, counts and their exact units must support every non-unknown statement. A working viewer alone is not a validated discovery engine.

Retain the original speaker, date if stated, instrument, plan route if stated, first-time/Groww/first-year fit, and the next scheduled payment result or `unknown`. Do not infer an outcome from an open SIP, holding, mandate approval, initial purchase or suggested workaround.
2. Add distinct public first-person accounts only where a source-specific collection/use route permits it. Keep App Store, Play Store, Reddit, social, YouTube, investing forums and other conversations as separate lanes. A family with zero analysed items stays visibly zero; a link alone never raises the count. The earlier 100–150 item target is a collection aim, not an insight threshold.

**Exit:** a source ledger with real counts and exact links; a varied set of real, permissible cases and the true shortfall from the approximate 20-item evaluation target. Assistant source checks are marked as assistant checks, never independent human verification. If no strict Groww account exists, keep that gap explicit.
