## PM reasoning that decides the scope

**Decision:** test an understanding-and-handoff aid for the automation-reliant routine restorer. **Consequential unknown:** does unclear current-versus-next status add avoidable repair work or unsafe assumptions beyond the underlying bank/rail failure? **Method:** compare a repeated repair account with a same-cycle recovery and informed adjustment; inspect current Groww help; compare current help, fixed guide and AI-assisted map on the same payment-understanding task. **Rivals:** execution remains broken despite clarity; existing help suffices; another SIP already keeps the person active; a pause was intentional. **Reversal:** drop the AI layer if the fixed guide matches it, or if the model creates false finality, duplicate-payment risk, advice or pressure; narrow the product claim if the cause is upstream execution.

The metric is `N=A+I+U` unique eligible people at month 12, with `A` requiring **at least one successful SIP instalment in the preceding 30 days**. A successful plan among several is one active person; a failed plan is not a lost person. The map addresses a **leading comprehension outcome**, and cannot calculate `A/N`, make an AutoPay repair, or estimate incremental retention. The five behavioral situations in the deck are research categories, not proven population clusters. The selection is reversible because reach, strict cohort fit, next scheduled outcome and business lift are unmeasured.

## What the product does now

The customer-facing page opens with one task. A tester chooses a sample or six observed states and gets a current/next payment map, conflict warning, neutral official checks and a fixed Groww help link. A de-identified free-text path is available as an experimental secondary route; its public model switch is off until independent real-item evaluation. When unavailable it sends the tester back to the working fixed choices. The application never stores the story, accesses an account, instructs a payment, recommends a fund or treats a deliberate pause as a defect. This is **functional prototype delivery with AI code held**, not fully validated AI-native efficacy.

## Required evidence still absent

The brief asks for 5–6 target interviews, 3 real target-user MVP task tests, and about 20 hand-labelled real AI cases with an agreement rate. Actual counts evidenced here are **0, 0 and 0**. Nine survey submissions and public posts help frame and challenge the hypothesis but cannot be renamed interviews. The AI model's one synthetic map run is a transport check. The earlier discovery model's failed extraction gate is reported in the Evidence Log. The submission therefore answers each PM decision in a bounded way and openly marks the unmet empirical requirements rather than manufacturing completion.
