- [VERIFIED] Target metric unit is an **eligible person**, not a plan, payment attempt, account, review or rupee. Several SIPs count once if any qualifying instalment succeeds. `N=A+I+U`: eligible mature people = confirmed active + confirmed inactive + unresolved. Public data cannot supply Groww's `N,A,I,U`. Source: [PART2_4_CURRENT_DECISION_2026-11-09.md](PART2_4_CURRENT_DECISION_2026-11-09.md).

## Glossary

- **SIP:** scheduled mutual-fund investment. **Due event:** scheduled instalment occasion. **Final success:** completed qualifying SIP order, distinct from bank debit or pending status. **Next scheduled outcome:** result at the following due event. **Cohort fit:** first-ever MF, first Groww SIP and first-year timing, each separately checked. **Source root:** one independent discussion/thread; replies are not independent roots. **Golden set:** independently human-labelled cases for measuring AI agreement. **Guardrail:** harm or failure metric that must not worsen while pursuing the North Star.
